About the Firm

From Strategy to Testimony. Los Angeles Forensic Accounting Experts

Established in Los Angeles in 1994, Engel & Engel LLP provides forensic accounting and advisory services in complex business litigation matters across California and nationwide.

We provide litigation support for plaintiffs and defendants in matters involving fraud investigations, economic damages, business valuation, bankruptcy and insolvency, and other financial matters.

Engel & Engel delivers industry-leading forensic accounting analysis and clear, defensible expert testimony in high-stakes matters nationwide. Engel & Engel’s forensic analysis has supported billions of dollars in court awards and defenses, and its professionals have been recognized nationally, including Forbes’ 2025 Top Valuation CPAs.
